Privacy Policy

Ulink Assist · Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how Ulink Assist ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you contact us through WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger or Instagram, and when our staff use our customer-support dashboard (the "Service") to reply to you.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact details such as your name, phone number, and your Facebook or Instagram profile identifier and display name, as provided by the messaging platform.
  • Message content: the text, images, documents and other files you send to us, and the replies we send to you.
  • Case information you choose to share with us (for example, appointment or enquiry details, and any health-related information you volunteer).
  • Technical records such as message timestamps and delivery or read status.

2. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request.
  • To arrange, confirm and follow up on appointments and cases.
  • To route and answer messages, which may include automated assistants. Staff can take over any conversation.
  • To keep the Service secure and to meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Meta platforms

Our Service connects to WhatsApp, Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts through Meta's official APIs. We receive only the messages and profile information that Meta provides for conversations you start with our accounts, and we use Page and account access tokens solely to read and reply to those messages. Meta's own handling of your data is governed by Meta's privacy policies.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the Service, such as cloud database, hosting, workflow-automation, messaging and AI language-model providers, and only as needed to provide the Service. We may also disclose information when required by law.

5. Storage and security

Information is stored in a cloud database with access restricted to authorised staff and systems. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

6. Retention

We keep conversations for as long as needed to provide the Service and meet our legal and business record-keeping needs, or until you ask us to delete them (see below).

7. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, at any time. You can also reply STOP to opt out of follow-up messages. To delete your data, see our Data Deletion Instructions or email supportdesk@ulinkassist.com.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

10. Contact

Ulink Assist — supportdesk@ulinkassist.com
