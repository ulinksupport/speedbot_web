Data Deletion Instructions

Ulink Assist · Last updated: 5 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal information we hold about you from conversations on WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger or Instagram.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to supportdesk@ulinkassist.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Include the phone number, or the Facebook / Instagram name you used to message us, so we can find your conversations. We may reply to confirm it is you.
  3. Or message us on the same channel you used and write “Please delete my data”.

What happens next

We will delete your conversations, contact details and related case records from our systems within 30 days of confirming your request, except for information we are required by law to keep. We will confirm by reply when it is done.

Removing the connection on Facebook / Instagram

You can also stop our access through Meta: in Facebook go to Settings & privacy > Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find the Ulink Assist integration and choose Remove. Then email us so we can delete the data already stored.

See also our Privacy Policy.
